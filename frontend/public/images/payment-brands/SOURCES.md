# Payment-brand artwork

Unmodified SVGs from [Active Merchant Payment Icons](https://github.com/activemerchant/payment_icons),
revision `fd43f66c17335fc167a37c1ef5080d3ebc222408`, retrieved 2026-11-19.
Source path: `app/assets/images/payment_icons/`.

Included: `visa.svg`, `master.svg`, `american_express.svg`, `discover.svg`,
`diners_club.svg`, `jcb.svg`, `unionpay.svg`, and `maestro.svg`.
The repository's MIT license is preserved in `LICENSE.txt`.
Brand marks belong to their respective owners. Original colors/proportions are
preserved. Artwork identifies the actual card brand returned by Braintree; it
does not advertise additional enabled payment methods. Assets are served locally,
without a runtime dependency or third-party image request.
